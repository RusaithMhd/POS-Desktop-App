1. THERMAL RECEIPT PRINTER (80mm & 58mm) SETUP:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TRIWYN POS features native direct ESC/POS hardware printing. It works with:
- EPSON TM-T88 / TM-T20 series
- Xprinter XP-N160M / XP-58 / XP-80 series
- Rongta RP80 / RP326 series
- Gprinter, Bixolon, POS-58, POS-80 USB/Network printers

Steps to Configure:
1. Connect your thermal printer to your Windows PC via USB cable.
2. Turn ON the printer and install the manufacturer's Windows printer driver.
3. Open Windows "Printers & Scanners" settings:
   - Ensure the printer appears and print a "Windows Test Page".
4. In TRIWYN POS:
   - Navigate to Settings -> Receipt & Hardware.
   - Choose your Receipt Width: 80mm (Standard) or 58mm (Compact).
   - Select your printer from the auto-detected System Printers dropdown.
   - Click "Test Print Receipt". The cutter should execute automatically.

2. CASH DRAWER (RJ11 / RJ12 KICK) SETUP:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Connect the standard RJ11/RJ12 cable from the cash drawer to the "DK" (Drawer Kick)
   port at the back of your thermal printer.
2. TRIWYN POS automatically sends the ESC/POS kick pulse (Pin 2 / Pin 5) whenever
   a Cash payment is completed.
3. To open manually: Press Ctrl + Shift + O in the POS terminal or click "Open Drawer"
   in the top navigation bar.

3. USB BARCODE SCANNER SETUP:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Plug any standard 1D/2D USB Barcode Scanner (Honeywell, Zebra, Datalogic, Eyoyo).
2. The scanner works instantly as a USB HID Keyboard emulation device.
3. Ensure the scanner is set to send a trailing "ENTER / CARRIAGE RETURN" suffix
   (standard factory default for 99% of barcode scanners).
4. When scanning any product barcode on the POS screen, the item is instantly
   added to the customer's cart.

4. DUAL CUSTOMER FACING DISPLAY (OPTIONAL):
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- If you have a secondary customer monitor, connect it via HDMI/VGA and set Windows
  display settings to "Extend Desktop".
- TRIWYN POS provides an optional Pop-out Live Customer Display window showing
  real-time subtotal, items scanned, and change due.
