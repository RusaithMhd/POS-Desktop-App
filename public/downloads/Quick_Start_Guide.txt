1. DAILY CASHIER WORKFLOW:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Launch Application
- Double click the "TRIWYN POS" icon on your Windows desktop.
- Log in with your assigned username and password (or PIN).

Step 2: Open Register Shift
- If a shift is not open, enter your Opening Cash Float (e.g. 5,000.00).
- Click "Open Shift". Your cash register drawer unlocks.

Step 3: Ringing Up Sales
- Scan product barcode with USB scanner, OR search by name / SKU in the search bar,
  OR tap product tiles in category grids.
- Modify item quantity or apply item-level discounts if authorized.
- Press F2 to quickly focus on the cart search.

Step 4: Tender & Payment
- Press F12 or click "Checkout / Pay".
- Select payment method: Cash, Card, Bank Transfer, or Split Payment.
- If Cash, enter amount received: the system calculates exact change due.
- Receipt automatically prints on your 80mm or 58mm thermal printer.

Step 5: Hold & Recall Carts (Queue Busting)
- If a customer needs to pick another item, click "Hold Cart" (Ctrl+H).
- Ring up the next customer.
- When ready, click "Recall Held Carts" to restore the previous transaction.

2. INVENTORY & BATCH MANAGEMENT:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Navigate to "Products & Inventory" (F4).
- Add products with SKU, Barcode, Cost Price, Selling Price, and Low Stock Alert threshold.
- Batch & Expiry Tracking: For FMCG or pharmaceuticals, assign Batch Numbers and
  Expiry dates. The system alerts when batches near expiration.

3. END OF DAY / CLOSING SHIFT:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Navigate to Shifts (F5) -> "Close Shift".
- Count actual cash, card slips, and vouchers in the drawer.
- The system generates an automated Z-Report comparing expected vs. actual totals.
- Print your Z-Report summary on the thermal printer.

4. LOCAL DATABASE BACKUPS (.posbak):
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Your data is stored locally in an embedded high-speed SQLite database.
- Go to File -> Backup Database (or press Ctrl + Shift + B).
- Save the backup file (`.posbak`) to a secure external USB drive or cloud drive.
