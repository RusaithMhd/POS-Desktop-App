TRIWYN POS — Windows Installation Guide

Complete step-by-step setup manual for Windows 10 & Windows 11 (64-bit)

1
Minimum System Requirements

Ensure your cashier terminal or computer meets these recommended specifications:

  • Operating System: Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit Edition)
  • Processor: Intel Core i3 / AMD Ryzen 3 or higher
  • Memory: 4 GB RAM minimum (8 GB recommended for busy retail stores)
  • Storage: At least 1 GB free SSD or HDD space
  • Display: 1366 × 768 or 1920 × 1080 resolution
2
Download & Extract the Setup Package

If you downloaded the complete ZIP package (TRIWYN-POS-Complete-Setup-Package.zip), right click the downloaded archive and select "Extract All...".

Files included in package:
├── POS-Setup.exe (Windows NSIS Setup Wizard)
├── Install_TRIWYN_POS.bat (Automated 1-Click Installer)
├── Installation_Guide.html (This document)
├── Hardware_and_Thermal_Printer_Setup.txt
├── Quick_Start_Guide.txt
└── WhatsApp_Activation_and_Support.txt
3
Execute POS-Setup.exe

Double click POS-Setup.exe or run Install_TRIWYN_POS.bat as administrator.

Windows SmartScreen Notice:
If Windows displays "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then click Run anyway to permit the installation.
4
Follow the Installation Wizard

The TRIWYN POS installer will prompt you:

  1. Accept the software terms & conditions.
  2. Choose the destination folder (default is recommended).
  3. Ensure "Create a desktop shortcut" is checked.
  4. Click Install. All files, offline SQLite databases, and native printer drivers will be copied.
5
Launch TRIWYN POS & Start Selling

Double click the TRIWYN POS icon on your desktop. The application launches immediately into the high-performance offline terminal interface.

Offline Protection: TRIWYN POS runs completely locally. Internet loss will never halt your checkout line.
6
Thermal Receipt Printer Configuration

Connect your 80mm or 58mm thermal printer via USB. Inside the application:

  1. Go to Settings → Receipt & Hardware.
  2. Select your thermal printer model from the dropdown.
  3. Click "Test Print" to verify cutter and print alignment.
  4. Connect your RJ11 cash drawer cable to the printer to enable automatic drawer opening on cash transactions.
